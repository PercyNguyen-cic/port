Fawcas

This is Fawcas, an original character of mine! Fawcas will walk you through some steps that were involved in his creation!

Initial Sketch

Start with an idea! I sketched out some inspirations on how his character was going to be like, elements I wanted to utilize in creating him, like the gauntlets and the fire.

The most important thing here is to get my ideas flowing onto the page first. Nothing rigid or clean, everything can be fixed up later.

Concrete Idea

This is where the the character starts to get fleshed out. Certain ideas are held onto or discarded. The colours are finalized, and the character comes to life!

Concept

Starts testing the character's personalities by putting them in scenecaps or short stories. This is to develop the character's abilities or mentality.

Whether he is strong or weak, stubborn or prevasive, straight forward or scheming, demonic or rightous, it shines through these few panels.

And we're done!
