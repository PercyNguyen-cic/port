Percy Nguyen

Illustrator & splash artist.

Percy (Phong) Nguyen is a professional illustrator and splash art artist, and runs his own art business. He studies UI/UX and Multimedia Design at Carleton University.

His work centres on characters: loud colour, strong silhouettes, and figures caught mid-moment.
