Character design studies

Shape Language

Reading personality from silhouette, shape and colour alone.

Designed to be read at a glance

Each of these characters was designed so you could guess their temperament before knowing anything about them. Rounded shapes, sharp angles, posture and palette do the talking.

The notes on each sheet show the decisions behind them: where the weight sits, which shapes repeat, and what the silhouette says first.
